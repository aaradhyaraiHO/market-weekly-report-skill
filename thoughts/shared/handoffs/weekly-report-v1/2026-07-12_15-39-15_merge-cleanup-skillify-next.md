## Learnings
- **`.git/info/exclude` hides `thoughts/` and `scripts` from `git status` in ALL worktrees.** Force-removing a worktree can silently delete uncommitted excluded files. Before any worktree removal: `git -C <wt> ls-files --others` (no exclude filters), or just tar the worktree first.

- Error: relative `cd scripts/weekly_report` failed after shell cwd reset → use absolute paths.
