*Weekly business reviews — what we learned and what we should test*
I spoke to/joined reviews across Italy, Iberia, Japan, North America and ANC; the format varies, but the same five jobs recur.
1. *Prioritize CEs:* Italy’s BGMs flag CEs before the call; NA reviews 20+ movers; Japan works directly from raw numbers. → Start with shared signal buckets, then let markets add custom flags.
2. *Gather stakeholder input:* Italy opens CE-level Slack threads and pulls in Perf/BDM/Ops; Iberia is also BGM-led with relevant owners answering async. → Make async input the default and Slack the interface.
3. *Align on actions:* Most markets do analysis before the call; Italy is moving toward a 30-minute review, while Japan handles more CE discussion live. → Use sync time only for unresolved/high-impact decisions.
4. *Track follow-through:* Asia relies on diligent manual follow-up; Italy has a WBR helper bot; elsewhere actions often resurface only the next week. → Keep CE-level to-dos with a DRI and scan Slack for completion.
5. *Build team context:* NA has “win of the week”; Iberia uses kudos; remote teams value seeing each other weekly. → Keep the weekly call, but protect time for wins, context and camaraderie.
*Proposed common review layer (not a prescribed meeting template):*
For every flagged CE, capture three things: (1) commentary/observations, (2) to-dos + DRI, and (3) items to revisit next week.
Allow BGM, Perf, BDM and Ops inputs; BGMs should not have to copy the same context between the report and a separate sheet.
Show a concise AI summary only inside the CE drawer/hover; weekly L1 and selective L2 context is useful, but automated full RCA will create noise.
After a call, process the Granola link, attach notes only on confident CE matches, and ask for a quick dropdown match for the rest.
Future inputs can add MMP handover, Salesforce/SP acquisition and Ops checks, without changing the core review flow.
*What we should validate with the broader team before building:* Are these the right five jobs, three fields and async/sync split for both large multi-BGM and small single-BGM markets?
